Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

At PadhaiNexa, we aim to provide a useful learning experience. Please read this Refund & Cancellation Policy carefully before purchasing any course or educational material.

1. Course Purchases

Before making a purchase, students should carefully review the course description, features, price, and other details displayed on the website.

2. Refund Requests

If you experience a payment issue, duplicate transaction, or have another purchase-related concern, please contact our support team with your registered email address and relevant transaction details.

3. Cancellation Requests

Students who wish to request cancellation should contact the support team and provide their course and transaction details. Each request will be reviewed according to the applicable purchase terms and policy.

4. Payment Failure

If money is deducted but the purchase is not confirmed, please contact our support team. Payment status will be checked with the payment provider before any resolution.

5. Refund Processing

If a refund is approved, it will be processed through the applicable payment provider. The time taken for the amount to appear in your account may depend on the payment provider and your bank.

6. Contact Support

For refund, cancellation, or payment-related questions, contact us:

Email: supportpadhainexa@gmail.com

Mobile: Contact the support number published on our Contact Us page.
